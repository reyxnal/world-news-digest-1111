World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026

1. Abu Dhabi talks. Russia, Ukraine and the United States held a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, their first trilateral meeting since February; President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called day one constructive (BBC, AP). Overnight Russian strikes hit power substations in 10 Ukrainian regions, killed 2 people and cut power as winter nears (Reuters).
2. Climate summit. World leaders opened the COP31 Antalya Leaders Summit in Antalya, Turkiye, on 11-12 November, hosted by President Recep Tayyip Erdogan under the theme of a sustainable, resilient and equitable future, with clean-energy and green-industry dialogues (AFP, UNFCCC).
3. Iran-linked plots. The US Justice Department charged 3 men described as Iran-backed over alleged plots including an incident at RAF Fairford, the UK air base used by US forces (BBC).
4. Ebola in Congo. Confirmed Ebola cases in the Democratic Republic of Congo passed 11,000, reaching 11,042 with 5,387 deaths in 8 of 26 provinces; the Bundibugyo-species outbreak, declared in mid-May, has no approved vaccine or treatment (Reuters).
5. US inflation and markets. US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October from September and core prices 0.3%, both above forecasts, reviving expectations of a December Federal Reserve rate rise; US stocks slipped while Japan's Nikkei 225 rose 2.87% to 60,459.58 and South Korea's Kospi rose 4.39% (BLS via reports, NDTV Profit).
6. US politics. The US House blocked a bid to impeach Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth, introduced by Rep. Thomas Massie; all House Republicans except Massie voted against it (BBC).
7. South Korea crash. At 07:34 in Seongnam, south of Seoul, a city bus hit 32 vehicles waiting at a traffic light, injuring 57 people, 9 seriously, none critically; the driver, in his 60s, said the brakes failed and tested negative for alcohol (Reuters, Yonhap).
